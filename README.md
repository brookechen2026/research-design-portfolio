# Research & Design Portfolio

Selected research presentations, posters, and interactive prototypes by Xiaoxi Chen, exploring education and human-centered technology. This repository brings together research and design artifacts. Collaborative work retains its team attribution. Materials illustrate project processes and outcomes.

## Turning Code into Compassion: An Aging Visualization Tool for Awareness and Reflection

A research project exploring personalized aging visualizations as a tool for awareness and reflection, developed through the National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA) SPIN program at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign under Dr. Deana McDonagh.

My work includes organizing biomedical evidence into a knowledge graph and connecting user profiles with prompts for personalized aging visualizations.

[View research materials](avatar/)

**Team:** Xiaoxi Chen, Vanessa Ng, and Zongheng Wu.
This folder contains presentation and poster materials. Research code is not included (cannot be public).

**Recognition:** For her contributions to this project, Xiaoxi Chen received the Fiddler Innovation Fellowship Undergraduate Award from NCSA in November 2026.

## ASL Aspire Redesign

A collaborative design project from CI499: Designing Inclusive Educational Technology, exploring multilingual accessibility, personalized learning pathways, progress tracking, and motivation for deaf and hard-of-hearing STEM learners.

The design was informed by related research, Universal Design for Learning, and a stakeholder interview.
